PRIVACY POLICY

Welcome to our platform. We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing life-saving community connection tools during emergencies. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, sign up for updates, or use our application.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your name, phone number, email address, and emergency contact details should be provided during sign-up.
  • Location Data: We use geolocation data when you submit a safety check-in, geotag a damage report, or request aid wherein location tracking is activated only with your explicit permission.
  • User Posts & Content: The application uses status updates, photos of road or damage hazards, mutual aid requests, and community messages posted on the public feed or interactive map.
  • Device & Network Data: This includes basic device type, operating system, and signal strength indicators to enable low-bandwidth and SMS fallback functionality.

How We Use Your Information

  • Emergency Coordination: Your information will be used display real-time safety check-ins, hazard reports, and mutual aid requests to nearby users and responders.
  • SMS Fallback: Your information will be used to deliver urgent notifications and maintain communication via SMS during low-bandwidth conditions.
  • Emergency Contact Notification: Your contact information will be used to alert your designated emergency contacts when you submit a safety check-in or request aid.

How We Share Your Information

  • Public Community Feed: Posts, hazard photos, and mutual aid requests shared on the public feed or map are visible to other users.
  • Emergency Responders: Relevant location and damage data may be shared with authorized local emergency services to assist in relief efforts.
  • Zero Commercial Selling: We never sell or rent your personal details, phone number, or emergency contacts to third parties.

How We Handle your Data Security

  • Encryption and Safeguards: We implement standard encryption and access controls to protect your personal data and location history.
  • Location Permissions: Geolocation tracking is strictly opt-in and can be disabled anytime via your device settings.
  • Account Control: You may update or permanently delete your account, posts, and emergency contacts at any time.
