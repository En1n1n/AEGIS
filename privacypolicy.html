PRIVACY POLICY

Welcome to our platform. We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing life-saving community connection tools during emergencies. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, sign up for updates, or use our application.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: The name, phone number, email address, and emergency contact details should be provided during sign-up.
  • Location Data: We use geolocation data when you submit a safety check-in, geotag a damage report, or request aid wherein location tracking is activated only with your explicit permission.
  • User Posts & Content: The application uses status updates, photos of road or damage hazards, mutual aid requests, and community messages posted on the public feed or interactive map.
  • Device & Network Data: This includes basic device type, operating system, and signal strength indicators to enable low-bandwidth and SMS fallback functionality.
