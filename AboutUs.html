About Us

Vision

To build a resilient world where every citizen stays connected, safe, and supported during any disaster.

Mission

To empower communities and emergency responders with the tools and information needed to effectively manage and mitigate the impact of disastersall while giving real-time, low-bandwidth communication tools that can streamline safety check-ins, crowdsource damage reports, and deliver rapid aid when every second counts.
