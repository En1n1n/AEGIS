FEATURES

Need to know what our website offers?

Dive into our features designed to keep you connected and safe during emergencies. Our team has worked hard to create a comprehensive solution that addresses the most pressing needs during crisis situations. Social media can be helpful but sometimes, it gets buried under other random posts. So, we introduce to you the key features of our applications:

Tap Safety Check-in

let family, friends and community members instantly know you are safe with a single tap of a button.

Interactive Damage Map

Crowdsourced feed with geotagged photos whosing blocked roads, fallen trees, flooding, and power outages.

SOS & Resource Matching

Post specific requests for help (food, water, medical aid, rescue) or offer spare resources/ shelter to neighbors in need.

Low Bandwidth Mode

Lightweight text-first status updates and SMS fallback for areas with weak or unstable mobile networks.

Other Features

Family and Group Locators

This is a feature that is private, and is using an invite-only feature that allows family members to track each other's live locations and battery statuses during a crisis. Within these circles, members can automatically share single-tap status pings, safe arrival pings and battery level alerts without broadcasting their private whereabout or personal situation to the public.

Local Safety Newsfeed

Our Hyper-Local newsfeed prioritizes posts based on real-time proximity and according to their locality. Users will be able to filter updates depending on the distance. This ranges from immediate neighbors to city-wide alerts. Moreover, this ensures that the user will only see verified safety pings, local hazard reports, and nearby mutual aid requests that are relevant to the user's immediate location.

Community Reactions

Instead of liking, loving or even laughing emoji reactions in the posts, our application features different types of reactions such as "I Can Help", "Confirmed Hazard", or "Situation Cleared". In this way, users that need help will be prioritized and the effect of the disaster or hazard will be controlled by the community.

Verified Agency Broadcasts and Official Announcements

This feature mitigates the spread of misinformation during crisis. The official updates from local government units such as Barangay Halls will be able to inform the public of their emergency services like evacuation orders. Important announcements will stay permanently pnned to the top of the feed, giving the citizens a reliable source of truth when seconds count. Moreover, the account of the officials will be verified as a reminder that the account is legitimate.
